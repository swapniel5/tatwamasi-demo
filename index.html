A useful first conversation

Start with the space you’re planning.

A homeowner should be able to share the essentials and understand what happens next. The options below are suggested enquiry prompts; the studio’s actual services and process still need confirmation.

01

What are you planning?

A full home, one room, or a specific area such as a kitchen or wardrobe.

02

Where is the project?

City or neighbourhood helps establish whether the project is within the studio’s service area.

03

What matters most?

Share priorities, an approximate budget and when you hope to begin—if you know them.

04

Agree a next step

The studio can confirm fit, availability and how it prefers to take a project forward.
